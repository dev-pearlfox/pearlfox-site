Frequently Asked Questions

The questions we hear most about Eagle View.

What platforms does Eagle View run on?

macOS and Windows, as a native desktop app.

Which mail providers does it work with?

Microsoft Outlook (via Microsoft Graph) and Gmail — you can connect either.

Does Eagle View read the content of my emails?

No. It only reads subject, sender, date, and read/flag status — never the body or attachments. See our Security & Privacy page for the full detail.

How does the free trial work?

You get 30 days free, activated with just your email — no credit card required. A trial key is emailed to you and activates the app locally.

What happens when the trial ends?

You'll need a license to keep using Eagle View. Licenses are available in multiple durations. See the app's own license screen for current options, or get in touch if you have questions.

Is my license tied to one device?

Yes — a license activates against a specific device. If you need to move to a new machine, contact us.

Where is my data stored?

Eagle View doesn't store a copy of your mail anywhere — it's fetched live from your provider each time. License and trial records (your email, a device identifier) are stored on our backend to validate your license.

I have another question — who do I ask?

Email us directly at dev@pearlfox.io — we read and respond to every message ourselves.
