Eagle View

  • Live Mail, To Do, and Meetings rows with a refresh interval you control
  • Works with both Microsoft Outlook and Gmail accounts
  • Five polished themes, built for a native, unobtrusive feel
  • Checklist, World Clock, Currency & Units converters, and more built in
  • Quick Links for one-click access to your most-used pages
  • Runs quietly at the edge of the screen — pin it, resize it, or tuck it away
