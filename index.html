Flagship Product

Eagle View

A lightweight desktop sidebar widget that keeps your Outlook and Gmail mail, meetings, and to-dos refreshed in real time — without ever needing to open your inbox.

  • Live Mail, To Do, and Meetings rows with a refresh interval you control
  • Works with both Microsoft Outlook and Gmail accounts
  • Five polished themes, built for a native, unobtrusive feel
  • Checklist, World Clock, Currency, and more built-in utilities
Why Eagle View

Built for people who live in their calendar and inbox all day, but don't want either one taking over their screen. Eagle View sits quietly at the edge, always current, never in the way.
