Eagle View

All your work, always in view.

Eagle View brings the parts of your workday you check constantly into one quiet, always-current sidebar.

  • Live Mail & To DoReal-time rows for mail and flagged items, refreshed on your own schedule.
  • Meetings & To Do, always currentYour next meeting, join link, and flagged items — without opening a calendar app or digging through mail.
  • Teams & Slack, right in your sidebarRecent chats and channel activity from both — unread counts, previews, and one click through to the conversation.
  • Jira & Azure BoardsAssigned tickets and work items with status pills — update transitions right from the sidebar, no browser needed.
